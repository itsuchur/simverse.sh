## 1. General Refund Rule

You may request a refund within **14 calendar days from the date of purchase**, provided that the purchased eSIM **has not been activated**.

The 14-day period does not guarantee a refund once the eSIM has been activated.

If the eSIM is activated before the end of the 14-day period, the eSIM becomes **non-refundable immediately**, except where applicable law requires otherwise or where the eSIM is defective due to a failure attributable to simverse or its supplier.

## 2. When an eSIM Is Considered Activated

An eSIM is considered activated when it reaches the activation state recorded by the relevant eSIM supplier, telecommunications provider, or mobile network.

Depending on the specific product, activation may occur:

- when the eSIM is installed;
- when the eSIM first connects to a supported mobile network;
- when a data session is first established;
- automatically at a time defined by the supplier; or
- under another activation condition stated in the product description.

The activation rules shown for the specific eSIM product take precedence where they provide more specific information.

The activation status recorded by the relevant supplier or network may be used to determine refund eligibility.

## 3. Electronic Delivery Does Not Automatically Cancel the Refund Right

Receiving an eSIM electronically, including receiving a QR code, activation code, installation details, or other provisioning credentials, does not by itself make the order non-refundable.

If the eSIM remains unactivated, you may still request a refund within 14 calendar days from purchase.

However, once the eSIM is activated, refund eligibility ends even if:

- fewer than 14 days have passed since purchase;
- little or no data has been used;
- the eSIM was activated accidentally;
- the User changed travel plans;
- the User no longer needs the eSIM; or
- the User activated the wrong eSIM.

## 4. Refunds for Unactivated eSIMs

A refund may be approved where all of the following conditions are met:

1. the refund request is submitted within 14 calendar days from the purchase date;
2. the eSIM has not been activated;
3. the eSIM has not otherwise been consumed or rendered non-refundable under supplier rules; and
4. the order can still be cancelled or invalidated by simverse or the relevant supplier.

Where these conditions are satisfied, simverse will normally cancel the eSIM and issue a refund.

## 5. Non-Refundable Situations

Unless required by applicable law, refunds will generally not be issued where:

- the eSIM has already been activated;
- the refund request is submitted more than 14 calendar days after purchase;
- the eSIM cannot be used because the User's device does not support eSIM;
- the device is carrier-locked or otherwise restricted;
- the User purchased an eSIM for the wrong country, region, duration, or data allowance;
- the User incorrectly installed or configured the eSIM;
- the User deleted the eSIM after activation;
- the User changed or cancelled travel plans;
- the User did not use the eSIM before its validity period expired;
- network quality, speed, signal strength, latency, or coverage is lower than expected but the eSIM otherwise functions within the advertised coverage conditions;
- the User cannot access a particular website, service, application, or online platform due to local, network, governmental, or third-party restrictions;
- the User requests a refund merely because data has not yet been consumed after activation; or
- the issue results from circumstances outside the reasonable control of simverse.

## 6. Defective or Non-Working eSIMs

If an eSIM does not work because of a technical failure attributable to simverse or its supplier, please contact support before deleting the eSIM from your device.

We may ask you to provide reasonable troubleshooting information, including:

- device model;
- operating-system version;
- screenshots of network settings;
- installation status;
- activation status;
- approximate location;
- error messages; and
- other diagnostic information reasonably necessary to investigate the issue.

Where the issue is confirmed to be attributable to simverse or its supplier, we may provide, as appropriate:

- troubleshooting assistance;
- a replacement eSIM;
- an equivalent eSIM product;
- account credit; or
- a refund.

Deleting the eSIM before troubleshooting is completed may prevent us from verifying the issue and may affect refund eligibility where the problem can no longer be reasonably investigated.

## 7. Device Compatibility

Before purchasing, you are responsible for verifying that your device:

- supports eSIM technology;
- is not carrier-locked;
- supports the required network technology;
- can install the purchased eSIM; and
- can be used in the intended destination.

An eSIM that remains unactivated may still qualify for a refund within the 14-day period.

However, if the eSIM has already been activated, device incompatibility does not restore refund eligibility.

## 8. Duplicate Purchases

If you accidentally purchase the same or substantially identical eSIM more than once, contact support as soon as possible.

A duplicate order may be refunded if the duplicate eSIM remains unactivated and the refund request is made within the 14-day refund period.

An activated duplicate eSIM is generally non-refundable.
